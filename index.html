What is UniMatch?

Not a search engine.
Not a chatbot. Something new.

UniMatch is a conversational AI that actually understands your situation — your budget, your country, your language, your goals — and matches you to universities that genuinely fit. No rankings to scroll through. No wrong data. No paradox of choice. Just 5–10 real options that make sense for you.

01
You describe your situation
Tell UniMatch what you want in plain language — country, budget, field, language, whatever matters to you. Vague or specific, it works either way.
02
It asks the right questions
UniMatch fills in the gaps with a few smart follow-up questions — never more than five, never anything unnecessary.
03
20+ filters applied automatically
Behind the scenes, UniMatch applies over 20 filters across a verified university database — tuition, deadlines, scholarships, language, city type, ranking, and more.
04
Real results, explained
You get 5–10 universities. Each with a match score, a plain-English reason why it fits, and direct links to apply. No hallucinated data. No guessing.
